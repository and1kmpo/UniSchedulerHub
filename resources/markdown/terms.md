# Terms of Service

TARRAYA provides academic operations features for higher education workflows, including enrollment, schedules, classroom usage, grading, reporting and identity access support.

Users must access the platform only with accounts and roles assigned to them. Attempting to access another user's academic information or restricted administrative areas is not permitted.

Academic records such as enrollments, grades and audit events are treated as operational history. They should be corrected through approved workflows rather than deleted informally.

Demo environments are provided for evaluation and portfolio review. Demo data is synthetic and should not be treated as official institutional data.

These terms are a product placeholder and should be reviewed by the deploying institution before any production use.
