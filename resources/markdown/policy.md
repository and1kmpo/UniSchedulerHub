# Privacy Policy

TARRAYA is an academic operations platform used to manage institutional workflows such as students, professors, programs, class groups, schedules, enrollments, grades and reports.

In demo or portfolio environments, the data is synthetic and exists only to validate product behavior. In a real institutional deployment, personal and academic data must be handled according to the institution's privacy, retention and security policies.

TARRAYA stores account, role and academic operation data needed to provide the service. Critical academic operations may be recorded in audit logs to support traceability, accountability and operational review.

Access to academic information is role-based. Students, professors, coordinators and administrators should only see the information required for their responsibilities.

This policy is a product placeholder and should be reviewed by the deploying institution before any production use with real personal data.
